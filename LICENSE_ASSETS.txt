Thunder Vector 3D starter assets

The original project assets under `game/assets/`, the two asset contact sheets
under `reference_images/`, the gameplay screenshots under `docs/screenshots/`,
the knowledge-graph diagrams, and the Godot primitive-mesh compositions were
created for this project. You may use, modify, and redistribute them as part of
your own project, including commercially.

The thirteen files under `game/assets/generated/*_imagegen_v1.png` are original
OpenAI ImageGen outputs created for this project: seven on 2026-08-29 and four
content-expansion assets on 2026-09-13, plus one wingman and one orbital planet
on 2026-10-04 (client date).
Their prompts, hashes, and image/alpha
verification methods are recorded in `game/assets/generated/ART_PROVENANCE.md`
and `game/assets/generated/CONTENT_ART_PROVENANCE.md`, plus
`game/assets/generated/ARCADE_ART_PROVENANCE.md` and
`game/assets/generated/MAP_ART_PROVENANCE.md`. The sector panorama is
intentionally opaque; the other twelve generated PNGs use transparency.

`game/assets/fonts/`: Noto Sans TC is third-party font software under SIL Open
Font License 1.1. Its unmodified notice is `docs/licenses/OFL_NotoSansTC.txt`;
source and exact hashes are in `game/assets/fonts/FONT_PROVENANCE.md`.
The grant also does not cover third-party software notices under
`docs/licenses/`, which remain under their stated licenses. Historical planning

`reference_images/gameplay_visual_target.jpg` and
